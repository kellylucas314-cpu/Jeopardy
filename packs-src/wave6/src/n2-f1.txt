## TEAM ORDERS | The teams on the Formula 1 grid
In 2021 the name of this British carmaker, James Bond's favorite, returned to the grid after 61 years => Aston Martin
In 2024 this British team in papaya orange won its first constructors' crown since 1998 => McLaren
In 2026 this General Motors luxury brand became the first brand-new team on the grid in a decade => Cadillac
This Volkswagen Group brand from Ingolstadt took over the Sauber team to enter Formula 1 in 2026 => Audi
In 2021 Renault renamed its F1 team for this sports-car brand of its own, itself named for mountains => Alpine
