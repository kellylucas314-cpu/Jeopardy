# Repeat audit, v4-nature drafts

Audited the 160 candidate pairs in `repeat-pairs-v4.txt`. A pair counted as a repeat when the NEW clue's hook was the same fact as the OLD clue, so a player who remembered the old clue would get the new one for the same reason. Two more same-fact repeats turned up while checking replacements: Halley's Comet in the Bayeux Tapestry (Fresh CROWNS & CHRONICLES) and von Frisch decoding the waggle dance (Fresh WORLD OF INSECTS). Every replacement's response and fact was grepped across public/data/original, easy, campaign, wave4 drafts and wave5 drafts.

Kept as different facts (not rewritten): penguin (dads warm the egg), monarch (milkweed), polio (iron lung), diamonds (Hope and Koh-i-Noor; the birthstone month is the category's gimmick), Maine coon, Krakatoa (red sunsets), Orion (Betelgeuse and Rigel), Adam's apple, Earth (1,000 inside Jupiter), Venus (243-day spin), stethoscope (Greek name), X-rays (Bertha Röntgen's hand), equator (doldrums), Gulf Stream (North Atlantic Drift), Pacific (Point Nemo), beavers (half-mile dam), blue (bowerbirds), Ramanujan (1729), Semmelweis reflex, Babbage (Difference Engine No. 2 in 1991), carbon dioxide (dry ice), woolly mammoths (Wrangel Island), Eiffel Tower (grows in heat), Pantheon (pumice dome), Rosalind Franklin (died before the Nobel).
