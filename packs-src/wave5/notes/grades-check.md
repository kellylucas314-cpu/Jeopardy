# Grades check: independent fact-check of the g1 to g8 edits

Scope: every category with an arrow in grades-g1.md to grades-g8.md (files g1 to g4 in
packs-src/wave4/drafts, g5 to g8 in packs-src/wave5/drafts). Pure row swaps got a ladder sanity
check only; every clue with new text was checked per CHECKER-BRIEF.md and QUESTION-QUALITY.md
(facts, single answer, response agreement, category and row fit, giveaways, length, evergreen,
banned phrases). 75 changed categories read; about 40 clues with new text plus the rewritten final
checked fact by fact. Both checkers print OK on wave4 and wave5 after the edits below.

## Changes (before → after, with the reason)

1. n1-w3-screen MOTOR CITY MUSIC $1000 (g1): "Motown got its first million-seller in 1960 with
   'Shop Around' by this group" → "'Shop Around,' a 1960 single by this group, became the Motown
   company's first million-seller" (the Miracles, unchanged). The single is from 1960 but it
   reached a million in sales in early 1961; the new wording dates the record, not the sales.
2. n1-w4-sci NUMBER CRUNCH TIME $1000 (g1): "...'Liber Abaci' introduced this Italian's famous
   sequence" → "...'Liber Abaci' brought this Italian's famous sequence to Europe" (Fibonacci,
   unchanged). The sequence was known to Indian mathematicians centuries earlier; the rabbit
   puzzle introduced it to Europe. 106 chars, bottom row, under the 110 cap.
3. e1-w1-hist KEEP THE HOME FIRES BURNING $800 (g3): "Air raid wardens patrolled to make sure no
   window glowed during these wartime drills" → "Wardens patrolled to make sure no window glowed
   during these wartime lights-out drills" (blackouts, unchanged). With "air raid" in the clue
   and "these wartime drills" as the ask, "air raid drills" was an equally natural response.
4. n1-v3-arts WIRELESS WONDERS $200 (g5): "This masked rider's adventures began on Detroit radio
   station WXYZ in 1933, with Tonto alongside" → "This masked rider galloped onto Detroit radio
   station WXYZ in 1933, and Tonto soon joined him" (The Lone Ranger, unchanged). The show
   premiered in January 1933 without Tonto; he was added a few episodes later that year.
5. n2-v2-world LIBERATORS & LAST EMPERORS $1200 (g6): "Brazil's monarchy ended with an 1889 coup
   that sent this emperor, on the throne since age 5, into exile" → "Brazil's monarchy ended with
   an 1889 coup that exiled this emperor, who had reigned since age 5" (Pedro II (Dom Pedro II),
   unchanged). Facts were right; the clue ran 103 chars on row 3, where the cap is 100. Now 95.
6. e1-v2-world MADE IN OLD CHINA $1000 (g7): "...beat a U.S. soldier's electric calculator" →
   "...beat a G.I.'s electric calculator" (the abacus, unchanged). Length only, 98 → 90, toward
   the Easy 50 to 85 target; the 1946 soroban-versus-calculator contest is as stated.

No responses, names, values, order or counts changed. "Shop Around", 'Liber Abaci', WXYZ, Pedro II
and "lights-out"/"blackout" were grepped against public/data/original, easy, campaign and both
drafts folders first; none appears elsewhere in the game.
