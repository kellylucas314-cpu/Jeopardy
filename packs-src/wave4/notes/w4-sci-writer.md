## Clues to check closely
- n1 IDEA FACTORY $1000 (Garrett Morgan): his 1923 signal patent did add an all-directions
  stop. Check the wording "all-way 'stop' position".
- n1 IDEA FACTORY $200 (drinking straw): Marvin Stone's 1888 patent and the rye-grass story
  are the usual account.
- n1 PERIODIC TABLE MANNERS $600 (iodine): reworded so it doesn't claim pure sublimation, just
  that warm crystals give off violet vapor.
- n1 GREEN THUMB DRIVE $600 (redwood): Hyperion is about 115.9 m. I avoided saying "tallest"
  because a taller tree could still be found.
- n1 AFTERS PARTY $800: Black Forest cake is "named for a German region". Some sources say it
  is named for the region's kirsch, not the region itself.
- n2 FRUIT BOWL $400: "world's largest herb" is the popular phrasing. More precisely it is the
  largest herbaceous flowering plant.
- n2 WHEY TO GO $1600: "Italy's best-known blue cheese" (Gorgonzola) is a soft superlative.
- n2 WHOSE FOOD IS IT ANYWAY? $2000 (Umberto I): the clue keeps "is said to" because the
  Margherita story is a legend.
- e1 PENNY CANDY COUNTER $1000: the story that the gum is pink because it was the only dye
  Diemer had is how it is usually told.
- e2 WHITE COAT WONDERS $800 (Salk): the church bells ringing on April 12, 1955 are widely
  reported.
- e2 WHO'S A GOOD DOG? $1200 (poodle): the clue says it was "first bred to fetch ducks out of
  the water", the usual account of its water-retriever origin. I took out the Germany-vs-France
  origin question.
- nf GUAC OF FAME: Rudolph Hass was a mail carrier who grew the mother tree from a seedling.
  The clue says "grown by" rather than "planted by".
