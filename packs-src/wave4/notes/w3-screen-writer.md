## Clues worth a second look
- n2 STATUESQUE PICTURES $2000 (Wings): "the inaugural top Oscar winner". At the first ceremony, Wings won Outstanding
  Picture and Sunrise won a separate artistic award. The Academy counts Wings as the first Best Picture, so I kept it.
- n2 CLASSIC ROCK ROLL CALL $2000 (Steely Dan): the "gadget" in 'Naked Lunch' is a sex toy. I worded it neutrally.
- n2 LONG-PLAYING BEATLES $1600 (Let It Be): released May 8, 1970. McCartney made the split public on April 10.
  Phil Spector is credited as producer, though Glyn Johns and George Martin worked on the sessions earlier.
- e1 PAIRED FOR LAUGHS $1000 (Martin and Lewis): "16 films" counts their starring features from 1949 to 1956 and
  leaves out their cameo in 'Road to Bali'.
- e1 SOCK HOP HITS $200 (Hound Dog): Thornton recorded it in 1952. Her hit came in 1953 and Elvis's in 1956.
- e1 HOLLYWOOD HOOFERS $400 (The King and I): Marni Nixon dubbed Deborah Kerr's singing. The clue only says the
  character whistles a happy tune.
- e2 GROOVY SIXTIES SOUNDS $1200: Sonny and Cher were not legally married until 1969, so the clue calls them "later TV
  hosts", not a married pair.
- nf ONE STEP AHEAD (HAL/IBM): the letter shift is real. Clarke and Kubrick denied it was on purpose, and the clue
  makes no claim either way.
- nf TAKE A HINT (Haydn, 'Farewell' Symphony): the story that the players put out their stand candles comes from
  the traditional account (Griesinger) and is the usual telling.
