## Changes made (before → after)
- FULL CIRCLE: "...this Englishman was the first captain to survive his own voyage around the world" → "Knighted on the Golden Hind in 1581, this Englishman was the first to command a round-the-world voyage from start to finish". Reason: factual error. Juan Sebastián Elcano took command of Magellan's expedition after 1521 and captained the Victoria home in 1522, so he, not Drake, was the first captain to survive a circumnavigation. Drake's genuine distinction is leading the whole voyage (1577–80) from departure to return. Response unchanged (Francis Drake), 122 chars.
- BACK FROM THE DEAD: "...this ancient language became official in the new state of Israel in 1948" → "...this ancient language has been an official tongue of Israel since 1948". Reason: Hebrew had already been an official language of Mandatory Palestine since 1922, so "became official in 1948" implied a first that isn't true; the new wording keeps the same fact without that implication. Response unchanged (Hebrew), 118 chars.
- MEDICAL IMMORTALITY: "this Virginia woman" → "this Virginia-born woman". Reason: Lacks was born in Roanoke and raised in Clover, Virginia, but had lived in Maryland since 1941 and the 1951 biopsy was at Johns Hopkins in Baltimore. Response unchanged (Henrietta Lacks), 123 chars.
