70 Finals, all rewritten to 70–130 chars as two-step riddles about famous subjects. Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings.

## Factual errors fixed
- PRESIDENTIAL MATH: "the number of presidencies exceeds the number of presidents by one" is no longer true (Trump is 45th and 47th). Rewrote around Cleveland losing to and then beating Benjamin Harrison; no count claim.
- WORTH ITS WEIGHT: saffron "has long rivaled gold" in price is false by an order of magnitude. Now "the world's costliest spice".
- BROADWAY RECORDS: "logged Broadway's longest run" is a breakable record (Chicago is closing in). Rephrased historically: "more than any Broadway show when it closed in 2023".
- THE MATH BEHIND PHYSICS: dropped the "1915 theorem" date (proved 1915, published 1918) and hung the clue on Einstein's 1935 eulogy instead.

## Duplicate responses removed (same answer twice in one Finals pack)
- LOW POINTS was a second Dead Sea (also BELOW SEA LEVEL). Now Death Valley: Badwater Basin, 282 ft below sea level, ~85 miles from Mount Whitney.
- LINES OF LATITUDE was a second Brazil (also TREE'S COMPANY). Now Ecuador: the Mitad del Mundo monument near Quito stands ~240 m off the true equator.
