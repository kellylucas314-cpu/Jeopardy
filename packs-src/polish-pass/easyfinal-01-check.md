## Changes made (before → after)
- V FOR VICTORY: "The BBC opened its wartime broadcasts with the 'da-da-da-DUM'..." → "The BBC opened wartime broadcasts to Europe with the 'da-da-da-DUM'..." Reason: the Fifth's opening was the station signal of the BBC European Service (the V campaign, from 1941), not of all BBC output; "its wartime broadcasts" overstated it. Response unchanged (Ludwig van Beethoven), 120 chars.
- HOLIDAY HISTORY: "An eight-night December festival, its name means 'dedication'..." → "An eight-night Festival of Lights, its name means 'dedication'..." Reason: Hanukkah begins in late November in a fair number of years (e.g. 2013, 2021), so "December festival" is not always true; "Festival of Lights" is its standard nickname and, with 'dedication' plus the nine-candle lamp, still points to exactly one answer. Response unchanged (Hanukkah), 103 chars.
