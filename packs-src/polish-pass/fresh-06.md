## Judgment calls / things I was less sure of
- Ethiopia: dropped "never fully colonized" (Italian occupation 1936-41 makes it arguable); kept the Kaldi coffee legend.
- Tweed: the cloth's name is probably a misreading of "tweel" rather than from the river, so the clue says only that it "shares a name with" the River Tweed, which is true either way.
- Salary: the "paid in salt" story is disputed; clue only says the word comes from Latin sal, which is the accepted etymology of salarium.
- St. Augustine: kept "oldest continuously occupied European settlement in the U.S."; San Juan (1521) is older but in a territory. Original said "continental U.S."; shortened for length. If you want belt-and-braces, restore "continental".
- Air Jordan: the NBA actually fined the Air Ship, not the Air Jordan I, so the clue is framed as "ads bragged the NBA had 'banned' its colors", which is accurate.
- Cleopatra "last active pharaoh": standard phrasing (Caesarion was nominal co-ruler); kept.
- Medici popes: changed "multiple" to "several" (Leo X, Clement VII, Pius IV, Leo XI); safe.
- Deuce from French "deux": the standard account (à deux de jeu); kept.
- Sandhill cranes "some 500,000": estimates run 500,000-650,000+; "some 500,000" is conservative.
- Duplicate answers remaining across the batch (seahorse, Mars, Venus, Wheel of Fortune, The Price Is Right, London) are all in different chunks/games, so left as is.
